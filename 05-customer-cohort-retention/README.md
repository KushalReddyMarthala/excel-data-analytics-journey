# 📊 Customer Cohort Retention Analysis Dashboard

### Online Retail | Monthly Customer Lifecycle & Retention Analysis

This is my final Excel analytics project, created as part of my journey from learning Excel fundamentals to applying Excel for practical data analysis.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze customer retention behavior by grouping customers into acquisition cohorts and tracking their purchasing activity over time.

The analysis helps answer questions such as:

- How many customers belong to each acquisition cohort?
- How many customers return after their first purchase?
- How does retention change month by month?
- Which cohorts show stronger long-term retention?
- How quickly does customer engagement decline over time?

| Month 3 Retention | 44.45% |
| Month 6 Retention | 26.21% |

## 🔍 Key Insights

- January 2011 is the largest customer cohort with 192 customers.
- Month 1 retention averages 55.91%, showing that more than half of customers returned during the following month.
- Month 3 retention averages 44.45%, showing a gradual decline in repeat engagement.
- Month 6 retention falls to 26.21%, indicating that roughly one quarter of customers remain active after six months.
- December 2010 is the only cohort with a complete 12-month observation window.
